3. **Functions, target audience and value**

   QOT is a Uganda-focused classifieds marketplace for adults who want to buy, sell or discover locally available goods and services. Users can browse ads without signing in, search by category and Ugandan location, save ads and searches, follow sellers, post and manage ads, chat with buyers and sellers, make price offers, review verified transactions and report unsafe or inappropriate content. QOT solves the problem of discovering and advertising local items in a simple, location-aware marketplace. QOT does not process payments, hold funds, arrange delivery or act as a party to transactions.

4. **Access instructions**

   - Launch QOT. The Home, Categories and Ads screens can be browsed without signing in.
   - To access account features, tap Profile, choose Sign In, select “Use email instead,” and use the review account credentials already supplied in App Review Information.
   - The review account is pre-verified. It can post ads, save ads and searches, follow sellers, send messages, make offers, report content and access account settings without receiving an SMS OTP.
   - To post an ad, tap the orange Post button, choose a category, add photos, complete the required details and location, preview the ad, and submit it.
   - Account controls, including freezing the account, are available under Profile > Settings.
   - No payment, purchase, subscription or paid content is required to access the app’s core features.

5. **External services, tools and platforms**

   - QOT API (`https://api.qot.ug`) for accounts, listings, media, chat, offers, moderation, reviews and notifications.
   - Google Sign-In for optional OAuth authentication.
   - Expo Notifications and Apple Push Notification service for device alerts.
   - Africa’s Talking for Uganda SMS/OTP delivery.
   - Apple iOS location, camera and photo-library services when the user chooses those features.
   - BigDataCloud reverse geocoding as an Android-focused fallback when a device’s native reverse geocoder does not return enough location detail.

   QOT does not use an external payment processor, AI service or delivery platform for its core marketplace flow.

6. **Regional behavior**

   QOT currently targets Uganda. Marketplace locations are Ugandan districts, cities and areas; prices are displayed in UGX; and phone-number verification currently supports Ugandan +256 numbers. The same core app functions are available wherever the app is installed, but relevant marketplace content and phone verification are Uganda-specific.

7. **Regulated services and protected third-party material**

   QOT is not a regulated financial, medical, gambling or transportation service, and it does not provide protected third-party media as part of its core functionality. Marketplace ads and images are user-generated. Users can report ads, sellers and chats, and can block other users. QOT is operated by Vimbah Computer Services Ltd.
